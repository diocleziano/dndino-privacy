# DnDino Hero

![DnDino Hero](../images/icons/dndino-hero-icon.png){ .img-logo }

**DnDino Hero** is the player app for managing a character sheet on iPad and iPhone.

It is designed to work with DnDino at the table, but it can also be used as a standalone digital character sheet.

## What It Does

DnDino Hero lets you manage:

- character identity
- species and background
- class, subclass, and multiclass progression
- ability scores, skills, and saving throws
- hit points, hit dice, rests, and conditions
- weapon attacks
- spells and spell slots
- feats
- equipment
- text notes and handwritten notes
- local connection with DnDino
- private chat with the DM

The goal is not to replace the rulebook. The app gives you a flexible sheet where you can enter official content, custom content, and campaign material.

## Sheet Philosophy

The sheet is intentionally generic.

Classes, species, feats, spells, and rules can be created, imported, duplicated, and reused as needed.

Reusable data is separated from the individual character:

- a **class** can be assigned to multiple characters
- a **species** can be reused without re-entering every trait
- a **background** can be selected by multiple sheets
- spells, feats, and rules can be imported and associated when needed

## Complete and Compact Sheet

DnDino Hero has two main sheet modes:

- **Complete Sheet**, richer and more detailed
- **Compact Sheet**, denser and designed to keep many values visible in limited space

You can choose the preferred mode from settings.

!!! tip
    The compact sheet is especially useful during play, when you want quick access to hit points, armor class, initiative, saving throws, attacks, spells, and resources.

## DnDino Connection

The connection with DnDino is local and intended for table play on the same network or nearby devices.

Hero can find an active DnDino session, connect to the DM's adventure, and send the active character. If a connection has already been made before, the sheet hero area shows a quick reconnect entry with the last DM and adventure.

The connection can synchronize:

- current, maximum, and temporary hit points
- armor class
- conditions
- spent hit dice
- Heroic Inspiration
- main character data sent to DnDino

When DnDino is in combat, updates apply to the combat participant and stay linked to the Hero sheet. When Hero returns to the foreground, it tries to reconnect and DnDino sends the current authoritative state back.

## Private DM Chat

When the character is connected to DnDino, the **Private Message to DM** section becomes available.

The chat is text-only, separated by character and device, and uses a bubble-style layout. In DnDino, the DM can open the chat from the connection panel, from the adventure character card, or from combat quick actions.

## Getting Started

Recommended flow:

1. create your character
2. fill in identity, species, and background
3. choose or create the class
4. complete ability scores, hit points, armor class, and initiative
5. add attacks, spells, feats, and equipment
6. enable iCloud backup if you want data protection
7. if you play with DnDino, connect the character to the DM's adventure

!!! note
    Content remains local to the device, except for iCloud backup features when enabled in settings.
